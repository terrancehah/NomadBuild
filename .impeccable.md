# Design Context

## Users

Chinese-speaking parents in the Cheras / Taman Connaught area of Kuala Lumpur, typically browsing on mobile. They're either referred by friends via WhatsApp or searching for local tuition options. They want to quickly assess whether 育人 is credible, what courses are offered, and how to contact the centre. The site needs to convert browsers into WhatsApp enquiries.

## Brand Personality

**Energetic, caring, human-first.** 育人 is not a cold corporate institution — it's a community tuition centre that genuinely cares about each student. The energy comes from confidence in results and pride in the teaching team. Think of it as an enthusiastic coach who's also a warm mentor.

## Aesthetic Direction

- **Tone:** Exciting and energetic. Strong color contrast is intentional — the brand red, navy, and yellow are meant to bring excitement, mirroring the bold poster aesthetic from their social media.
- **Theme:** Light mode primary, dark mode fully supported.
- **Fonts:** Noto Sans SC (body), ZCOOL KuaiLe (poster headlines), Anton (teacher names), FengYun (brand name). These are deliberate choices tied to the client's identity.
- **Brand colors:** Primary red (#BF391F), secondary navy (#1F365D), accent yellow (#FFD400), sky cyan (#3AA0C0) — all sampled from real social media posts.
- **Poster primitives:** The red-frame + yellow-highlight + outlined-headline aesthetic from their social media should appear at key moments (hero, CTA) but not overwhelm the whole site.

## Design Principles

1. **Energy through contrast** — bold color pairings and confident typography, not through gimmicks or animations.
2. **Human warmth** — real photos, real teacher profiles, real testimonials. The site should feel like people, not a template.
3. **Clarity for parents** — Chinese-speaking parents scanning on mobile need clear hierarchy, fast load, and obvious WhatsApp CTAs.
4. **Small but sharp** — improvements should be targeted and purposeful, not sweeping redesigns. Every change earns its place.
5. **Poster moments, not poster everything** — save the loud branding energy for hero sections and CTAs; let content sections breathe.
